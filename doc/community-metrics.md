# Community metrics

[English](community-metrics.md) · [Español](metricas-comunidad.es.md)

This record measures real use and collaboration. Stars, views, and downloads provide context but
do not count as a successful installation, pilot, or contribution.

## Definitions

- **Confirmed installation:** someone other than `fernando-delrio` reports that Equipo Solo was
  installed and the seven agents appeared.
- **Pilot team:** a team explicitly agrees to test `lead-flow` and reports its experience.
- **Claimed issue:** an external person is assigned to an issue or publicly says they are working on it.
- **External pull request:** a pull request authored by someone other than `fernando-delrio`.
- **First response time:** elapsed time from an external issue, pull request, or discussion to the
  first maintainer response. Report the median when at least one response exists.
- **Returning participant:** an external person contributes on more than one date or thread.

Do not record names, client data, private repositories, or private conversations. Keep only counts
and links to public evidence. A release-asset download is not a confirmed installation.
